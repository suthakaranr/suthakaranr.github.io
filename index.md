I am currently an Associate Professor in the Department of Mathematics at California State University, San Bernardino. I earned my Ph.D. in Statistics from Bowling Green State University under the supervision of [Prof. Wei Ning](https://www.bgsu.edu/arts-and-sciences/mathematics-and-statistics/faculty-and-staff/wei-ning.html) in 2020. I received my M.A. in Mathematics from the University of Nebraska at Omaha, advised by [Prof. Steven From](https://www.unomaha.edu/college-of-arts-and-sciences/mathematics/about-us/directory/steven-from.php). I obtained my B.Sc. (Hons) in Statistics and Operations Research and Postgraduate Diploma in Applied Statistics from the University of Peradeniya in 2010 and 2013, respectively.

My research interests include change point analysis, sequential data analysis, statistical inference, and high-dimensional data analysis. I enjoy working with undergraduate and graduate students on research projects &mdash; see the [Research](pages/Research.html) page for current areas of interest and a full list of publications.
